# Grounded ad creative

Establish platform, placement, format, audience awareness, offer, objective and available performance data. Check current official placement specifications before delivering production-ready lengths or dimensions; upstream numeric tables may age.

Use winning ads, reviews, comments, approved product evidence and brand assets as a grounded corpus. Each substantive concept should trace to a source or an explicitly labeled creative hypothesis. If a corpus is missing, use known product facts for a reviewable draft and ask only for evidence needed to make a claim; do not invent performance history or stop all useful work.

Choose genuinely different angles: specific pain, desired outcome, mechanism, real objection or supported proof. Vary the concept before varying synonyms. Build the hook, main message, evidence and action around one angle. Preserve approved phrasing and accurate product limitations.

For batch delivery, assign stable concept/variant IDs; include angle, format, text, asset pointers, source of the claim and checked character counts. Independently combinable headlines must make sense alone and in the permitted combinations. Format outputs for the requested platform or review artifact.

For motion, UGC, faceless or static formats, choose based on message and usable assets, not a universal format ranking. A staged customer exchange or review must not masquerade as an actual testimonial.

Iterate from spend, impressions, clicks and conversions with dates and attribution context. Keep a control and isolate the hypothesis being changed. Low sample size, conversion lag and delivery differences limit conclusions; a fixed impression threshold is not universal statistical sufficiency. Generating or reviewing ads does not authorize purchasing traffic or uploading campaigns.
