# Prose editing

Read the complete text under review once when structural editing requires it; a narrowly selected passage is enough for a local correction. Mark strong patterns first: a paragraph that delays its claim, contrasts without a real disputed alternative, empty importance claims, repetitive section closers or whole passages addressed to the wrong reader.

Rewrite around the claim rather than swapping isolated forbidden words. Remove formulaic triads only when their third item adds nothing. Keep useful parallelism, technical terminology and deliberate punctuation. Adapt observations to the actual language rather than mechanically applying English word bans to Russian.

Compare before/after for facts, names, numbers, dates, sources, causal claims, rankings, simultaneous events and uncertainty. A smoother sentence that changes chronology or certainty is an error. Authoritative exact passages may be placed beside a proposed revision for review rather than silently overwritten.

Read the result for rhythm and coherence. Deliver one finished version by default. Show intermediate diagnostics only for an explicit editorial review; repeated draft/critique/final blocks waste context during an embedded writing task.
