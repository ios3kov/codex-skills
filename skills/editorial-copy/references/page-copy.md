# Marketing page copy

Establish the primary action, audience awareness, product promise, differentiator, traffic source and evidence. Missing customer research limits specificity; it does not justify fabricated outcomes. Draft conservative copy from known product facts and flag proof still needed.

Translate features into concrete reader outcomes, keeping causal claims proportionate to evidence. Use customer language from real reviews or interviews without manufacturing quotations. One section should advance one argument.

Build the page around its purpose:

| Page | Copy emphasis |
|---|---|
| Homepage | Who it serves, what it does, useful paths to deeper information |
| Landing page | Continuity with the traffic source, one offer/action, relevant proof and objections |
| Pricing | Scope, cost, limits, included features and decision criteria |
| Feature/product | Capability, use cases, operation and supported benefits |
| About | Verifiable story, people and reason to trust |

Make the headline understandable without a clever reveal. The subheadline clarifies audience, mechanism or outcome. CTA copy names the action and its result; nearby text resolves actual friction. Do not add guarantees, scarcity or social proof without authorization and evidence.

Keep ad promise, page promise and product behavior consistent. When useful, supply a few meaningfully different headline options within one artifact. General conversion figures from the source are not performance promises for this product; effectiveness needs a real experiment.
