# Local reference research fallback

The provider, capture and synthesis references are bundled. Refero, Mobbin, Scenario, Details and Design Spells are optional discovery services, not owned asset collections or required engines.

Without a provider, work from the user's supplied images, approved project assets, existing local reference collections and the product itself. Keep source/version, inspected properties, rights evidence and missing coverage explicit. Reconstructed flows are proposals, not observations. When no references are available, offer an original direction grounded in the brief and label it as such; do not claim a live competitor inspection.

For each useful owned reference capture the original file, a stable asset ID, date, source credit, usage permission, observed mechanism and intended adaptation. Package those approved files with the project, not just their URL. Do not bulk-copy paid galleries, personal data or unlicensed third-party assets to fill this skill. Current provider terms/access still govern a new live task; if unavailable, use local evidence and preserve the gap.
