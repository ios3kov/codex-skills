# Faithful logo application in 3D and motion

## Source contract

Start from the approved vector/path or supplied 3D master, not a visual imitation of its render. Record source version and permitted extrusion, bevel, material, light, camera and motion. Preserve the approved 2D master; production copies and 3D derivatives have separate identities.

With only raster input, inspect resolution/distortion. Vector reconstruction is approximate until compared and accepted; never claim exact curves recovered from pixels. If exact geometry is required, obtain the master or identify the limitation while continuing independent look development.

Use actual modeling/rendering when a mesh, editable scene or geometrically controlled render is required. A generated raster treatment may serve look exploration or a requested illustration; it cannot prove topology/source preservation. Do not promise precision the available workflow cannot verify.

## Add volume, preserve the mark

Import/reuse source contours and verify them before depth. Inspect units, uniform scale, orientation, reflected axes, transforms, grouping, holes and winding/fill behavior. Preserve symbol/wordmark relationship, letter spacing and negative spaces; do not replace lettering with a similar font.

Extrusion adds depth to the source footprint. Check that bevels, smoothing, remeshing, subdivision or displacement do not inflate silhouettes, close counters, round intentional corners, move joins or create intersections. Choose a smaller/inset bevel or omit it when the footprint must remain unchanged. Expose necessary geometry changes instead of hiding them with lighting.

Set tessellation/curve resolution by visible contour accuracy at target scale. Inspect caps, sidewalls, normals, seams, facets, intersections and shadow artifacts. Do not automatically convert to meshes or merge components when retaining curves/separate source shapes better preserves the asset.

## Verify geometry separately from beauty

Produce a neutral proof: front-aligned orthographic view, flat opaque silhouette, no perspective, glow, shadows, displacement or material effects. Align with a rasterization of the master using the same crop, scale and sampling. Overlay contours or compare masks; inspect outer edges, counters, letter gaps and critical landmarks. Uniform alignment may correct framing; local warping must not hide drift.

Separate sampling/antialiasing from construction differences. State measurement method, resolution and agreed tolerance where measurements help. Global overlap can hide a lost narrow counter or letter feature; inspect those individually. Do not invent a universal similarity threshold. For exact source geometry, retain a traceable source-contour mapping and inspect model operations too; silhouette pixels alone do not prove path equality.

Perspective can change apparent shape in a beauty render. Keep intended projection separate from the neutral proof and compare the same approved source/version, not a historical screenshot.

## Materials, light and rendering

Choose materials/light from the brief and surface. Verify plausible scale, thickness, roughness/reflections and readable critical edges/counters. At delivery resolution inspect highlights, shadows, noise, jagged contours, texture stretching, shading seams, transparency fringes and environmental contact. Denoising must not erase letter details.

Material color under illumination need not equal a palette hex value in final pixels. For exact color delivery define viewing/output conditions, use color-managed references and check exported artifacts. Preserve an approved flat-color variant separately when physical materials cannot reproduce that appearance.

## Animation and handoff

Animate camera, light or rigid transforms when those meet the brief. Anchor parts to the approved lockup. Nonuniform stretching, re-spacing and morphing change the mark: use them only when the requested motion direction permits, with a verified readable hold/end state as required. Reveal/assembly effects need checks through movement, not only at the final frame.

Use `motion-storytelling` for pacing, camera and normal-speed review; the applicable renderer skill owns implementation/export. Inspect frames and playback for clipping, changing letter gaps, collapsed counters, temporal noise, flicker, unintended blur and requested loop seams.

Deliver requested source/model/scene/render formats with canonical reference, treatment settings and proof views. Report separately: source geometry, neutral proof, beauty render, animation/playback. A setup or single still cannot certify unrendered animation.

For Blender, verify import, curve geometry and camera behavior against documentation matching the installed version before implementation. Tool settings are not universal identity rules.
