# Brand System

Work at the requested level: concept, refinement, assessment, application or production. An identity board communicates a direction; it does not establish that a usable vector master or verified 3D asset exists.

## Choose the relevant module

- [Logo design](references/logo-design.md): concept families, shape, negative space, lettering, competitor comparison, size tests and delivery assets.
- [Logo in 3D and motion](references/logo-3d-motion.md): preserve an approved master through modeling, materials, lighting and animation; verify geometry separately from appearance.
- [Identity application](references/identity-application.md): connect the approved logo, palette, typography and image language across the requested touchpoints.
- For positioning and competitive offers, use [editorial-copy brand positioning](references/shared/editorial-copy/references/brand-positioning.md). This skill owns visual identity, not a second strategy workflow.
- For shared type rendering, use [interface-craft typography](references/shared/interface-craft/references/typography.md). Logo-specific letter drawing and lockup spacing belong to the logo module.

Read only material needed for the task. Reuse existing guidelines, source assets and project records. Preserve the exact name, letterforms and approved variants when applying an identity. A critique can recommend a redesign; it does not authorize replacing the master. A requested new identity or redesign authorizes the corresponding design work without extra routine approval steps.

## Establish the source and scope

Identify the canonical asset/version from supplied files, brand records and actual usage; do not automatically assume a navigation logo, favicon or press kit is current. Resolve conflicting versions before claiming fidelity. Distinguish working assumptions from confirmed constraints and continue work that does not depend on missing information.

For a new logo, connect alternatives to the audience, name, purpose and intended character. For an existing logo treatment, begin with its source geometry. Keep concept exploration, constructed artwork, presentation and verified delivery distinct.

## Delivery and verification

Deliver requested assets/applications with source pointers, named variants and the checks actually performed. Keep editable masters and distributable files distinct. Use established asset locations; do not create competing brand documents or overwrite approved originals.

Use `interface-craft` for web implementation, the requested document/deck skill for its format, `motion-storytelling` for timing and shots, and `media-production-control` for acceptance/provenance. Use raster generation/editing tools for raster deliverables where appropriate; an attractive generated image alone cannot prove vector or mesh fidelity.

Report geometry, appearance, recognition research and export/playback coverage separately. Missing tooling or source quality limits the affected check, not all independent work. Provenance and adaptation boundaries: [sources.json](references/sources.json).
